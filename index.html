Privacy Policy — NEON TILT RACING

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Developer: PA Gaming Studio

PA Gaming Studio developed NEON TILT RACING as a mobile racing game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use our game.

1. Information Collection

NEON TILT RACING does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number.

The game integrates Google AdMob, which may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers
  • Advertising interactions and app activity
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Approximate location inferred from technical information, where applicable

2. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information to deliver advertisements, measure their effectiveness, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services.

For more information, visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Motion Sensors

NEON TILT RACING uses device motion sensors for tilt-based steering. Sensor readings are processed locally for gameplay and are not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

4. Game Progress

Game achievements and progress are stored locally on the user's device. We do not operate a separate account system for storing this information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not independently sell users' personal information. Our advertising provider, Google AdMob, may collect and share information according to its applicable policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control. Third-party services apply their own security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

NEON TILT RACING is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and data processing are subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, advertising services, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

Developer: PA Gaming Studio

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the game's Google Play Store listing.
